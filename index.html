Shortcuts

Drag to move · drag a corner to resize · drag the top dot to rotate (Shift snaps) · Delete removes · Ctrl+D duplicates · [ and ] change the order · arrow keys nudge · Ctrl+A selects all · keys 1 to 7 pick a tool.

Cut out

Drag to trace the shape you want to tear out, or click to place corner points. Straight lines between points tear like a hand-ripped edge.

Crop from a PDF

Drag a box around each clipping. Take as many as you like from any page; click a box to drop it.

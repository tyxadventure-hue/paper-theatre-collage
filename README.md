# Paper Theatre Collage

A browser tool for making digital collage material look physical: torn and cut paper, paper stocks, prints in a small set of inks, and wear such as rips, creases, sun bleach and water damage.

Bring in your own photos (or crop them from a PDF), tear or cut them, print them in a shared set of inks, age them, and export them as transparent PNGs or as layered PSD and AI files for Procreate and After Effects.

**Open it:** https://tyxadventure-hue.github.io/paper-theatre-collage/

## How it works

- Everything runs in your browser. Your pictures are never uploaded anywhere.
- Work is autosaved in the browser. Use **Files → Save project** to keep a project file.
- It is a single file, `index.html`, with no build step.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Photos | Add photos, crop clippings from a PDF, patterned papers, flat silhouettes |
| Scissors | Tear or cut edges, cut circles and bursts, hand-cut outlines |
| Paper | Paper stocks, and blank sheets in any colour |
| Print | Type and stamp plates, printed by hand-press processes |
| Wear | Rips, folds, light and water damage, on one piece or the whole board |
| Ink | The board's ink sets, previewed on your own picture |
| Files | PNG, layered PSD and AI export, and project files |
